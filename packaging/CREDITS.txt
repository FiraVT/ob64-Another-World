LordlyCaliber, by rempred (MIT license)
    https://github.com/rempred/LordlyCaliber

    An editor for Ogre Battle 64 that ships a partial decompilation of the
    game. This project does not run LordlyCaliber; it uses its research, and
    the port imports LordlyCaliber projects.

    The overhaul exists because of it. Before this project found
    LordlyCaliber, the work had reached the new field controls and stopped
    there: the game's data was not understood well enough to change what the
    enemy fields. LordlyCaliber's decompiled C, record formats and validator
    rules supplied that understanding. Built directly on it:

    - Custom enemy units and spawning: the war director writes the game's
      35-byte enemy unit record (a leader and two member groups of up to
      three). Garrisons, bought units, boss escorts and supply trains are all
      built this way.
    - Enemy weapons: the item choice in each member group of that record,
      and the rule that a choice replaces the class's basic weapon in the
      same slot (LordlyCaliber's squads.js). The enemy weapon upgrades are
      built on it.
    - Save data: field names in the save table (hpMax and its neighbours)
      that the field screens read.
    - The game's code: names for 1,070 of the game's functions
      (tools/revmap.py, tools/lc_lookup.py), and the decompiled routines used
      to understand the field orders and the town liberation rule.
    - Sprites: the format the Organize screen draws portraits with.

    Its license is reproduced at the end of this file.
